# Aria Volt: Brochure

> SYNTHETIC document created for a training project. Aria Volt is a fictional vehicle; all figures are invented.

## Overview
A practical electric SUV for daily use. The Aria Volt is a 5-seat compact SUV sold in the upper-mid price band, with four trims (Base, Mid, High and Top) starting at an ex-showroom price of about INR 1,250,000. A flat floor gives the rear passengers extra foot room. Fast-charging support restores most of the battery in about an hour.

## Design and Comfort
The Volt has 400 litres of boot space and 197 mm of ground clearance. It seats 5 people. Available colours are Sandstone Beige, Midnight Black, Solar Orange, Forest Green. The upper trims add leatherette upholstery and ambient interior lighting.

## Safety
The Volt has a synthetic safety rating of 5 out of 5 stars. All trims include dual front airbags and ABS with EBD. Six airbags are standard across the range. Electronic stability control with hill-hold is part of the Top trim.

## Powertrain and Efficiency
The Volt is powered by a 21.2 kWh battery with a claimed range of about 182 km on a full charge. The electric motor produces 133 PS and 289 Nm of torque.

## Technology and Features
Trims build on one another:

- **Base** (Automatic): adds the essential safety and comfort equipment.
- **Mid** (Automatic): adds Steering-mounted audio controls, Rear AC vents, Electrically adjustable mirrors, Rear power windows, Bluetooth connectivity, DC fast-charging support.
- **High** (Automatic): adds Reverse parking camera, Automatic climate control, Side and curtain airbags, Cruise control, Push-button start with smart key, Touchscreen infotainment (9-inch) with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, DC fast-charging support.
- **Top** (Automatic): adds Leatherette upholstery, LED headlamps with DRLs, Electronic stability control with hill-hold, Ambient interior lighting, Rear defogger, Tyre pressure monitoring, Electric sunroof, DC fast-charging support.

## Ownership
Aria offers a 4-year or 120,000 km warranty on the Volt. Road tax is charged at roughly 2% of the ex-showroom price in this catalogue's pricing model, and insurance at about 3.5%.

## Who It Suits
The Volt suits buyers who want a compact SUV that avoids fuel stops for daily commuting. It is best suited to small families and city or mixed driving.
