# Kiran Kinetic: Brochure

> SYNTHETIC document created for a training project. Kiran Kinetic is a fictional vehicle; all figures are invented.

## Overview
A premium electric SUV with long range. The Kiran Kinetic is a 5-seat SUV sold in the premium price band, with four trims (Base, Mid, High and Top) starting at an ex-showroom price of about INR 2,450,000. Dual-motor power gives quick acceleration and good traction. A heat-pump air-conditioning system protects range in hot and cold weather.

## Design and Comfort
The Kinetic has 488 litres of boot space and 214 mm of ground clearance. It seats 5 people. Available colours are Solar Orange, Midnight Black, Pearl White, Ocean Blue. The upper trims add leatherette upholstery and ambient interior lighting.

## Safety
The Kinetic has a synthetic safety rating of 5 out of 5 stars. All trims include dual front airbags and ABS with EBD. Six airbags are standard across the range. Electronic stability control with hill-hold is part of the Top trim.

## Powertrain and Efficiency
The Kinetic is powered by a 24.4 kWh battery with a claimed range of about 209 km on a full charge. The electric motor produces 261 PS and 627 Nm of torque.

## Technology and Features
Trims build on one another:

- **Base** (Automatic): adds the essential safety and comfort equipment.
- **Mid** (Automatic): adds Steering-mounted audio controls, Rear AC vents, Electrically adjustable mirrors, Rear power windows, Bluetooth connectivity, DC fast-charging support.
- **High** (Automatic): adds Reverse parking camera, Automatic climate control, Side and curtain airbags, Cruise control, Push-button start with smart key, Touchscreen infotainment (9-inch) with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, 360-degree camera, DC fast-charging support.
- **Top** (Automatic): adds LED headlamps with DRLs, Electronic stability control with hill-hold, Ambient interior lighting, Rear defogger, Tyre pressure monitoring, Electric sunroof, 360-degree camera, DC fast-charging support.

## Ownership
Kiran offers a 5-year or 150,000 km warranty on the Kinetic. Road tax is charged at roughly 2% of the ex-showroom price in this catalogue's pricing model, and insurance at about 3.5%.

## Who It Suits
The Kinetic suits buyers who want a SUV that avoids fuel stops for daily commuting. It is best suited to small families and city or mixed driving.
