# Aria Terra: Brochure

> SYNTHETIC document created for a training project. Aria Terra is a fictional vehicle; all figures are invented.

## Overview
The all-rounder compact SUV. The Aria Terra is a 5-seat compact SUV sold in the mid price band, with four trims (Base, Mid, High and Top) starting at an ex-showroom price of about INR 820,000. Wide rear doors make loading children and luggage straightforward. A turbo-petrol option gives it surprising pace.

## Design and Comfort
The Terra has 393 litres of boot space and 199 mm of ground clearance. It seats 5 people. Available colours are Pearl White, Flame Red, Solar Orange, Forest Green. The upper trims add leatherette upholstery and ambient interior lighting.

## Safety
The Terra has a synthetic safety rating of 4 out of 5 stars. All trims include dual front airbags and ABS with EBD. Side and curtain airbags arrive from the High trim. Electronic stability control with hill-hold is part of the Top trim.

## Powertrain and Efficiency
The Terra uses a 1469 cc petrol engine producing 110 PS and 153 Nm of torque. Claimed fuel efficiency is 18.5 km/l. The fuel tank holds 47 litres.

## Technology and Features
Trims build on one another:

- **Base** (Manual): adds the essential safety and comfort equipment.
- **Mid** (Manual): adds Touchscreen infotainment (7-inch), Steering-mounted audio controls, Rear AC vents, Electrically adjustable mirrors, Rear power windows, Bluetooth connectivity.
- **High** (Manual): adds Alloy wheels, Reverse parking camera, Automatic climate control, Side and curtain airbags, Cruise control, Push-button start with smart key, Touchscreen infotainment (9-inch) with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto.
- **Top** (Automatic): adds Wireless phone charger, Leatherette upholstery, LED headlamps with DRLs, Electronic stability control with hill-hold, Ambient interior lighting, Rear defogger, Tyre pressure monitoring, Electric sunroof.

## Ownership
Aria offers a 3-year or 100,000 km warranty on the Terra. Road tax is charged at roughly 10% of the ex-showroom price in this catalogue's pricing model, and insurance at about 3.5%.

## Who It Suits
The Terra suits buyers who want a compact SUV that balances comfort and capability. It is best suited to small families and city or mixed driving.
