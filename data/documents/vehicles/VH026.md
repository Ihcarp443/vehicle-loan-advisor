# Surya Sarathi: Brochure

> SYNTHETIC document created for a training project. Surya Sarathi is a fictional vehicle; all figures are invented.

## Overview
A premium people mover. The Surya Sarathi is a 7-seat MPV sold in the upper-mid price band, with four trims (Base, Mid, High and Top) starting at an ex-showroom price of about INR 1,790,000. Captain seats in the second row come with powered recline on the top trim. Rear-cabin comfort is the focus, with sunblinds and a rear-seat entertainment option.

## Design and Comfort
The Sarathi has 277 litres of boot space and 187 mm of ground clearance. It seats 7 people. Available colours are Sandstone Beige, Forest Green, Ocean Blue, Solar Orange. The upper trims add leatherette upholstery and ambient interior lighting.

## Safety
The Sarathi has a synthetic safety rating of 5 out of 5 stars. All trims include dual front airbags and ABS with EBD. Six airbags are standard across the range. Electronic stability control with hill-hold is part of the Top trim.

## Powertrain and Efficiency
The Sarathi uses a 1516 cc diesel engine producing 124 PS and 295 Nm of torque. Claimed fuel efficiency is 17.9 km/l. The fuel tank holds 51 litres.

## Technology and Features
Trims build on one another:

- **Base** (Manual): adds the essential safety and comfort equipment.
- **Mid** (Manual): adds Steering-mounted audio controls, Rear AC vents, Electrically adjustable mirrors, Rear power windows, Bluetooth connectivity.
- **High** (Manual): adds Alloy wheels, Reverse parking camera, Automatic climate control, Side and curtain airbags, Cruise control, Push-button start with smart key, Touchscreen infotainment (9-inch) with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto.
- **Top** (Automatic): adds Wireless phone charger, Leatherette upholstery, LED headlamps with DRLs, Electronic stability control with hill-hold, Ambient interior lighting, Rear defogger, Tyre pressure monitoring, Electric sunroof.

## Ownership
Surya offers a 4-year or 120,000 km warranty on the Sarathi. Road tax is charged at roughly 12% of the ex-showroom price in this catalogue's pricing model, and insurance at about 3.5%.

## Who It Suits
The Sarathi suits buyers who want a MPV that balances comfort and capability. Families who need three rows of seating will find it a good fit.
