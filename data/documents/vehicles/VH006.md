# Zenith Zap: Brochure

> SYNTHETIC document created for a training project. Zenith Zap is a fictional vehicle; all figures are invented.

## Overview
The affordable electric city car. The Zenith Zap is a 5-seat hatchback sold in the mid price band, with four trims (Base, Mid, High and Top) starting at an ex-showroom price of about INR 860,000. Instant torque makes stop-start traffic effortless. Regenerative braking has three selectable levels, including near one-pedal driving.

## Design and Comfort
The Zap has 251 litres of boot space and 175 mm of ground clearance. It seats 5 people. Available colours are Sandstone Beige, Solar Orange, Pearl White, Midnight Black. The upper trims add leatherette upholstery and ambient interior lighting.

## Safety
The Zap has a synthetic safety rating of 4 out of 5 stars. All trims include dual front airbags and ABS with EBD. Side and curtain airbags arrive from the High trim. Electronic stability control with hill-hold is part of the Top trim.

## Powertrain and Efficiency
The Zap is powered by a 20.2 kWh battery with a claimed range of about 173 km on a full charge. The electric motor produces 80 PS and 159 Nm of torque.

## Technology and Features
Trims build on one another:

- **Base** (Automatic): adds the essential safety and comfort equipment.
- **Mid** (Automatic): adds Touchscreen infotainment (7-inch), Steering-mounted audio controls, Rear AC vents, Electrically adjustable mirrors, Rear power windows, Bluetooth connectivity, DC fast-charging support.
- **High** (Automatic): adds Reverse parking camera, Automatic climate control, Side and curtain airbags, Cruise control, Push-button start with smart key, Touchscreen infotainment (9-inch) with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, DC fast-charging support.
- **Top** (Automatic): adds Leatherette upholstery, LED headlamps with DRLs, Electronic stability control with hill-hold, Ambient interior lighting, Rear defogger, Tyre pressure monitoring, DC fast-charging support.

## Ownership
Zenith offers a 3-year or 100,000 km warranty on the Zap. Road tax is charged at roughly 2% of the ex-showroom price in this catalogue's pricing model, and insurance at about 3.5%.

## Who It Suits
The Zap suits buyers who want a hatchback that avoids fuel stops for daily commuting. It is best suited to small families and city or mixed driving.
