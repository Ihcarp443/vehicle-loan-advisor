# Surya Shaurya: Brochure

> SYNTHETIC document created for a training project. Surya Shaurya is a fictional vehicle; all figures are invented.

## Overview
A rugged mid-size SUV. The Surya Shaurya is a 5-seat SUV sold in the upper-mid price band, with four trims (Base, Mid, High and Top) starting at an ex-showroom price of about INR 1,490,000. Low-range capability and hill-descent control are included on upper trims. The rear suspension is tuned for heavy loads.

## Design and Comfort
The Shaurya has 549 litres of boot space and 203 mm of ground clearance. It seats 5 people. Available colours are Ocean Blue, Forest Green, Flame Red, Sandstone Beige. The upper trims add leatherette upholstery and ambient interior lighting.

## Safety
The Shaurya has a synthetic safety rating of 4 out of 5 stars. All trims include dual front airbags and ABS with EBD. Side and curtain airbags arrive from the High trim. Electronic stability control with hill-hold is part of the Top trim.

## Powertrain and Efficiency
The Shaurya uses a 2003 cc diesel engine producing 155 PS and 389 Nm of torque. Claimed fuel efficiency is 15.3 km/l. The fuel tank holds 60 litres.

## Technology and Features
Trims build on one another:

- **Base** (Manual): adds the essential safety and comfort equipment.
- **Mid** (Manual): adds Touchscreen infotainment (7-inch), Steering-mounted audio controls, Rear AC vents, Electrically adjustable mirrors, Rear power windows, Bluetooth connectivity.
- **High** (Manual): adds Alloy wheels, Reverse parking camera, Automatic climate control, Side and curtain airbags, Cruise control, Push-button start with smart key, Touchscreen infotainment (9-inch) with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, 360-degree camera.
- **Top** (Automatic): adds Leatherette upholstery, LED headlamps with DRLs, Electronic stability control with hill-hold, Ambient interior lighting, Rear defogger, Tyre pressure monitoring, Electric sunroof, 360-degree camera.

## Ownership
Surya offers a 4-year or 120,000 km warranty on the Shaurya. Road tax is charged at roughly 12% of the ex-showroom price in this catalogue's pricing model, and insurance at about 3.5%.

## Who It Suits
The Shaurya suits buyers who want a SUV that balances comfort and capability. It is best suited to small families and city or mixed driving.
