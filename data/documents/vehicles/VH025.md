# Veda Voyager: Brochure

> SYNTHETIC document created for a training project. Veda Voyager is a fictional vehicle; all figures are invented.

## Overview
Comfortable and capable on long trips. The Veda Voyager is a 7-seat MPV sold in the upper-mid price band, with four trims (Base, Mid, High and Top) starting at an ex-showroom price of about INR 1,240,000. The diesel engine suits fully loaded highway journeys. A tall roofline gives the third row decent headroom.

## Design and Comfort
The Voyager has 275 litres of boot space and 183 mm of ground clearance. It seats 7 people. Available colours are Sandstone Beige, Midnight Black, Solar Orange, Titanium Grey. The upper trims add leatherette upholstery and ambient interior lighting.

## Safety
The Voyager has a synthetic safety rating of 4 out of 5 stars. All trims include dual front airbags and ABS with EBD. Side and curtain airbags arrive from the High trim. Electronic stability control with hill-hold is part of the Top trim.

## Powertrain and Efficiency
The Voyager uses a 1642 cc diesel engine producing 102 PS and 291 Nm of torque. Claimed fuel efficiency is 17.8 km/l. The fuel tank holds 52 litres.

## Technology and Features
Trims build on one another:

- **Base** (Manual): adds the essential safety and comfort equipment.
- **Mid** (Manual): adds Touchscreen infotainment (7-inch), Steering-mounted audio controls, Rear AC vents, Electrically adjustable mirrors, Rear power windows, Bluetooth connectivity.
- **High** (Manual): adds Alloy wheels, Reverse parking camera, Automatic climate control, Side and curtain airbags, Cruise control, Push-button start with smart key, Touchscreen infotainment (9-inch) with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto.
- **Top** (Automatic): adds Wireless phone charger, Leatherette upholstery, LED headlamps with DRLs, Electronic stability control with hill-hold, Ambient interior lighting, Rear defogger, Tyre pressure monitoring.

## Ownership
Veda offers a 4-year or 120,000 km warranty on the Voyager. Road tax is charged at roughly 12% of the ex-showroom price in this catalogue's pricing model, and insurance at about 3.5%.

## Who It Suits
The Voyager suits buyers who want a MPV that balances comfort and capability. Families who need three rows of seating will find it a good fit.
