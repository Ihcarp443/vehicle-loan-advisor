# Veda Vista: Brochure

> SYNTHETIC document created for a training project. Veda Vista is a fictional vehicle; all figures are invented.

## Overview
A three-row SUV for large families. The Veda Vista is a 7-seat SUV sold in the upper-mid price band, with four trims (Base, Mid, High and Top) starting at an ex-showroom price of about INR 1,750,000. The third row has its own AC vents and USB charging points. Second-row captain seats are available on the top trim.

## Design and Comfort
The Vista has 490 litres of boot space and 205 mm of ground clearance. It seats 7 people. Available colours are Ocean Blue, Titanium Grey, Flame Red, Pearl White. The upper trims add leatherette upholstery and ambient interior lighting.

## Safety
The Vista has a synthetic safety rating of 5 out of 5 stars. All trims include dual front airbags and ABS with EBD. Six airbags are standard across the range. Electronic stability control with hill-hold is part of the Top trim.

## Powertrain and Efficiency
The Vista uses a 2018 cc diesel engine producing 162 PS and 370 Nm of torque. Claimed fuel efficiency is 14.5 km/l. The fuel tank holds 59 litres.

## Technology and Features
Trims build on one another:

- **Base** (Manual): adds the essential safety and comfort equipment.
- **Mid** (Manual): adds Steering-mounted audio controls, Rear AC vents, Electrically adjustable mirrors, Rear power windows, Bluetooth connectivity.
- **High** (Manual): adds Alloy wheels, Reverse parking camera, Automatic climate control, Side and curtain airbags, Cruise control, Push-button start with smart key, Touchscreen infotainment (9-inch) with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, 360-degree camera.
- **Top** (Automatic): adds Leatherette upholstery, LED headlamps with DRLs, Electronic stability control with hill-hold, Ambient interior lighting, Rear defogger, Tyre pressure monitoring, Electric sunroof, 360-degree camera.

## Ownership
Veda offers a 4-year or 120,000 km warranty on the Vista. Road tax is charged at roughly 12% of the ex-showroom price in this catalogue's pricing model, and insurance at about 3.5%.

## Who It Suits
The Vista suits buyers who want a SUV that balances comfort and capability. Families who need three rows of seating will find it a good fit.
