# Zenith Zephyr: Brochure

> SYNTHETIC document created for a training project. Zenith Zephyr is a fictional vehicle; all figures are invented.

## Overview
Long-distance comfort with diesel economy. The Zenith Zephyr is a 5-seat sedan sold in the mid price band, with four trims (Base, Mid, High and Top) starting at an ex-showroom price of about INR 1,050,000. The diesel engine pulls strongly from low revs, which makes overtaking relaxed. Highway stability is a strong point thanks to a wide track.

## Design and Comfort
The Zephyr has 499 litres of boot space and 168 mm of ground clearance. It seats 5 people. Available colours are Flame Red, Midnight Black, Titanium Grey, Pearl White. The upper trims add leatherette upholstery and ambient interior lighting.

## Safety
The Zephyr has a synthetic safety rating of 4 out of 5 stars. All trims include dual front airbags and ABS with EBD. Side and curtain airbags arrive from the High trim. Electronic stability control with hill-hold is part of the Top trim.

## Powertrain and Efficiency
The Zephyr uses a 1498 cc diesel engine producing 107 PS and 242 Nm of torque. Claimed fuel efficiency is 23.1 km/l. The fuel tank holds 45 litres.

## Technology and Features
Trims build on one another:

- **Base** (Manual): adds the essential safety and comfort equipment.
- **Mid** (Manual): adds Touchscreen infotainment (7-inch), Steering-mounted audio controls, Rear AC vents, Electrically adjustable mirrors, Rear power windows, Bluetooth connectivity.
- **High** (Manual): adds Alloy wheels, Reverse parking camera, Automatic climate control, Side and curtain airbags, Cruise control, Push-button start with smart key, Touchscreen infotainment (9-inch) with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto.
- **Top** (Automatic): adds Wireless phone charger, Leatherette upholstery, LED headlamps with DRLs, Electronic stability control with hill-hold, Ambient interior lighting, Rear defogger, Tyre pressure monitoring.

## Ownership
Zenith offers a 4-year or 120,000 km warranty on the Zephyr. Road tax is charged at roughly 12% of the ex-showroom price in this catalogue's pricing model, and insurance at about 3.5%.

## Who It Suits
The Zephyr suits buyers who want a sedan that costs little to run. It is best suited to small families and city or mixed driving.
