# Zenith Zenor: Brochure

> SYNTHETIC document created for a training project. Zenith Zenor is a fictional vehicle; all figures are invented.

## Overview
A performance-oriented luxury SUV. The Zenith Zenor is a 5-seat SUV sold in the premium price band, with four trims (Base, Mid, High and Top) starting at an ex-showroom price of about INR 2,550,000. A turbocharged engine and an eight-speed automatic deliver strong, smooth acceleration. Sport mode sharpens throttle, steering and suspension together.

## Design and Comfort
The Zenor has 453 litres of boot space and 207 mm of ground clearance. It seats 5 people. Available colours are Sandstone Beige, Midnight Black, Flame Red, Pearl White. The upper trims add leatherette upholstery and ambient interior lighting.

## Safety
The Zenor has a synthetic safety rating of 5 out of 5 stars. All trims include dual front airbags and ABS with EBD. Six airbags are standard across the range. Electronic stability control with hill-hold is part of the Top trim.

## Powertrain and Efficiency
The Zenor uses a 1998 cc petrol engine producing 169 PS and 282 Nm of torque. Claimed fuel efficiency is 14.4 km/l. The fuel tank holds 58 litres.

## Technology and Features
Trims build on one another:

- **Base** (Manual): adds the essential safety and comfort equipment.
- **Mid** (Manual): adds Steering-mounted audio controls, Rear AC vents, Electrically adjustable mirrors, Rear power windows, Bluetooth connectivity.
- **High** (Manual): adds Alloy wheels, Reverse parking camera, Automatic climate control, Side and curtain airbags, Cruise control, Push-button start with smart key, Touchscreen infotainment (9-inch) with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, 360-degree camera.
- **Top** (Automatic): adds Leatherette upholstery, LED headlamps with DRLs, Electronic stability control with hill-hold, Ambient interior lighting, Rear defogger, Tyre pressure monitoring, Electric sunroof, 360-degree camera.

## Ownership
Zenith offers a 5-year or 150,000 km warranty on the Zenor. Road tax is charged at roughly 10% of the ex-showroom price in this catalogue's pricing model, and insurance at about 3.5%.

## Who It Suits
The Zenor suits buyers who want a SUV that balances comfort and capability. It is best suited to small families and city or mixed driving.
