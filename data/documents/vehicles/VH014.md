# Nirvan Nexa CNG: Brochure

> SYNTHETIC document created for a training project. Nirvan Nexa CNG is a fictional vehicle; all figures are invented.

## Overview
A compact SUV that costs very little to run. The Nirvan Nexa CNG is a 5-seat compact SUV sold in the mid price band, with four trims (Base, Mid, High and Top) starting at an ex-showroom price of about INR 890,000. Dual interlinked CNG cylinders free up usable boot space. It remains one of the few SUVs of its size offered with factory CNG.

## Design and Comfort
The Nexa CNG has 311 litres of boot space and 192 mm of ground clearance. It seats 5 people. Available colours are Solar Orange, Titanium Grey, Sandstone Beige, Flame Red. The upper trims add leatherette upholstery and ambient interior lighting.

## Safety
The Nexa CNG has a synthetic safety rating of 3 out of 5 stars. All trims include dual front airbags and ABS with EBD. Side and curtain airbags arrive from the High trim. Electronic stability control with hill-hold is part of the Top trim.

## Powertrain and Efficiency
The Nexa CNG uses a 1197 cc cng engine producing 72 PS and 98 Nm of torque. Claimed fuel efficiency is 25.3 km/kg. The fuel tank holds 42 litres.

## Technology and Features
Trims build on one another:

- **Base** (Manual): adds the essential safety and comfort equipment.
- **Mid** (Manual): adds Touchscreen infotainment (7-inch), Steering-mounted audio controls, Rear AC vents, Electrically adjustable mirrors, Rear power windows, Bluetooth connectivity.
- **High** (Manual): adds Alloy wheels, Reverse parking camera, Automatic climate control, Side and curtain airbags, Cruise control, Push-button start with smart key, Touchscreen infotainment (9-inch) with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto.
- **Top** (Automatic): adds Wireless phone charger, Leatherette upholstery, LED headlamps with DRLs, Electronic stability control with hill-hold, Ambient interior lighting, Rear defogger, Tyre pressure monitoring.

## Ownership
Nirvan offers a 3-year or 100,000 km warranty on the Nexa CNG. Road tax is charged at roughly 8% of the ex-showroom price in this catalogue's pricing model, and insurance at about 3.5%.

## Who It Suits
The Nexa CNG suits buyers who want a compact SUV that costs little to run. It is best suited to small families and city or mixed driving.
